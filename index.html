Residents Only • Cash in on your empty spot. Lock in visitor parking

Enter your building's invite code and your email, and we'll send you a one-time sign-in link. No password needed.

WHAT WE COLLECT: To run Stallr for your building, we collect and store: your email address (for sign-in and booking notifications), your first name and last initial (shown to neighbours you interact with), your phone number when you book or list a spot (shared with the other neighbour on that booking as soon as a booking request exists between you, so you can coordinate before payment), your guest's license plate when you book a spot (shared with that spot's owner), your parking spot number if you list one (kept private until a booking is paid and confirmed), your Interac e-transfer email if you list a spot or book one (shared only with the other neighbour on that booking, so a refund can be sent if ever needed), and the timing/history of your listings and bookings.

WHO SEES WHAT: Your name and license plate are only ever shared with the specific neighbour you're transacting with for that booking — never posted publicly or shown to the whole building. Your phone number and Interac email are shared with that same specific neighbour once a booking request exists between you, so you can confirm with each other before any money moves.

NOT COLLECTED: We don't collect or store any actual payment details — all payments happen directly between neighbours via Interac, outside this app.
